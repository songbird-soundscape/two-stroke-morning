Find your local council member

Leaf blower hours, noise rules and gas-equipment phase-outs are mostly local decisions, made by a city or town council, or by county commissioners outside city limits.

1. Search your town's council pageSearch for "your town city council" (or "your county commissioners"). The official site lists the members and usually has a "find my ward" or "find my district" map, so you can find the one who represents your street.
2. Not sure which government is yours?USA.gov keeps a directory of state, county and city governments: usa.gov/local-governments. For state and federal officials: usa.gov/elected-officials.
3. Or call the city clerkThe clerk's office can tell you who represents you, how to email them, and how to sign up to speak during public comment at a council meeting (usually 2–3 minutes).
4. Then write to themTwo-Stroke Morning drafts the letter from your own street's numbers: noise at the window, the health effects, the fuel and the CO₂, plus one clear ask. Print it, sign it, and bring a copy to the meeting.
